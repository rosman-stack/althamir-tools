Perfumes and decants, explained simply

A decant is a small portion of an original perfume transferred to a smaller container. It's a practical way to try fragrances, especially Arabian and niche ones, without buying the full bottle. These tools help you decide which scent suits you, what it really costs to wear it and how to combine it.

They're made by Althamir GT, a decant and Arabian perfume shop in Guatemala. If you want to see what's in stock or request a perfume you can't find, visit the shop or message us on WhatsApp: we personalize every order.
