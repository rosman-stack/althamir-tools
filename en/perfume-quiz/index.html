How the quiz works

You answer three questions: what occasion you want the scent for, in what climate or time of day you'll wear it, and which family of scents appeals to you most. Your answers are then compared with fifteen signature scents, from citrus and marine to florals, leathers and orientals with oud, and we show you the three that fit best, with their top, heart and base notes, a guide on how to wear it and example perfumes.

Fragrance families at a glance

  • Fresh and citrus: lemon, bergamot and aquatic notes. Light, ideal for the heat.
  • Clean and soft: green tea, musk and clean laundry. Discreet and comfortable.
  • Woody and spicy: cedar, pepper and lavender. Elegant and versatile.
  • Floral and elegant: jasmine, rose and iris. Bright and sophisticated.
  • Sweet and gourmand: vanilla, date and praline. Warm and enveloping.
  • Oriental: amber, oud and resins. Intense and long-lasting.

Frequently asked questions

What are top, heart and base notes?

They're the three stages of a perfume. The top is what you smell in the first few minutes, the heart appears afterward and defines the character, and the base is what stays on your skin for hours.

Are the example perfumes for sale?

The examples are references so you can recognize the style; they don't mean they are in stock. The brands mentioned belong to their respective owners. If you want to know which decants are available, visit the shop or message us on WhatsApp.

Can I retake the quiz?

Of course. Change the weather or the occasion and you'll see other recommendations: many people prefer different scents by day and by night. Even with the same answers you may see different options, and each result shows you two more to explore.
