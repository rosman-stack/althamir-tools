How much does each spray cost?

Work out the real cost of smelling great: per spray, per day, and how long your bottle lasts.

How the cost per spray is calculated

A perfume is measured in milliliters, and each milliliter yields about 10 sprays on average. This calculator multiplies the milliliters in the bottle by 10 to estimate the total number of sprays, then divides the price by that number. That gives you the cost of each spray, what you spend per day depending on how many you wear, and how many days the bottle lasts.

A quick example

A 10 ml decant that costs Q70 yields about 100 sprays, or roughly Q0.70 per spray. If you wear 5 sprays a day, you spend about Q3.50 daily and the decant lasts around 20 days.

Frequently asked questions

Is the calculation exact?

It's an estimate. Real-world yield changes with the atomizer, how hard you press and the type of bottle, which is why we use an average of 10 sprays per milliliter.

Why compare a decant with a full bottle?

A decant lets you try a perfume for a fraction of the bottle's price. With the calculator you can see what each spray costs in both formats before you decide.

Can I use another currency?

Yes. Choose quetzales, dollars or euros; the math is the same because it only depends on the price you enter.

How many sprays should I wear per day?

It depends on the fragrance and the occasion. Many people wear between 3 and 6; stronger perfumes usually need fewer.
