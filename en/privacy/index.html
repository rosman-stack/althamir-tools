Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This site is operated by Althamir GT, a decant and Arabian perfume shop in Guatemala. For any question about this policy you can contact us through the About and contact page.

What information we use

The tools on this site (perfume quiz, cost-per-spray calculator and layering) run in your browser. What you type or choose in them, such as prices, sizes or quiz answers, is not sent to our servers or stored.

As on any website, the hosting provider may record standard technical data, such as the IP address, browser type and pages visited, for operational and security purposes.

External links and WhatsApp

Some buttons take you to our shop or open a WhatsApp conversation with a prefilled message that includes the tool's result. It is only sent if you decide to send it. When you leave this site, the privacy policies of those services apply, such as WhatsApp's (Meta).

Cookies and advertising

This site may display Google AdSense ads. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and other identifiers to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. The use of advertising cookies allows Google and its partners to show you ads based on your browsing.

  • You can manage or turn off ad personalization in Google's ad settings.
  • You can see how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and at business.safety.google/privacy.
  • You can also opt out of the use of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

If you visit the site from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we will show you a message asking for your consent before using advertising cookies, and you will be able to change your choice at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us for information about the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to us through the contact methods listed above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site's tools or the services we use change. The date above indicates the latest review.
