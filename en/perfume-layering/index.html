What perfume layering is

Layering means combining two fragrances to create a scent of your own. You choose a base (woody, oriental with oud, or amber with vanilla) that gives longevity and depth, and an accent (citrus, fruity, floral or gourmand) that adds freshness, juiciness or sweetness.

How to do it well

  • Start with the base and apply it on pulse points, like the neck and wrists.
  • Add the accent on top or in another area, like the chest or clothing.
  • Use few sprays at first: you can always add, but you can't take away.
  • Test the blend a few hours before an important event.

Frequently asked questions

Can any two perfumes be mixed?

Almost always, but it works best when they complement each other: a dense base with a lighter accent. This tool shows you 12 combinations designed to balance each other.

Why does the base go first?

It's the densest fragrance and the one that takes longest to evaporate; applied first, it sets the foundation of the blend, and the accent brightens it.

Are decants good for trying combinations?

Yes. They're an affordable way to try blends before buying full bottles.
