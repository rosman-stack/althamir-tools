Originality guarantee

Last updated: November 2026

What we sell

All Althamir GT perfumes are original products of their brand. An Arabian perfume smelling similar to a designer one doesn't make it an imitation: Lattafa, Armaf, Afnan and the others are brands with their own products. What we guarantee is that each product comes from the brand named on its box.

How we source

We buy from local and international suppliers that we verify beforehand as distributors of original perfumery. We work with several suppliers and keep a record of every product we ship.

What we check

Before shipping a product we check the box and its printing, the bottle, the sprayer, the labels and the authenticity details each brand includes, such as seals, holograms or official verification when they exist.

Full bottles

For full bottles the checking process is recorded and, if you ask, we share a short video with you.

Decants

Decants are prepared from the original bottle. Since a decant is only a portion, it does not carry the bottle’s batch code.

Our guarantee

If it is shown that a product we sold you is not original, for example an imitation or copy, we exchange it for the same original product. You have 15 days from the day you receive it to report it.

How to claim

Message us on WhatsApp within those 15 days and send photos of the product, the box and the bottle details, such as the batch code, the sprayer and the labels. We review your photos and the product you received and compare them with our record of what we shipped.

How to verify it yourself

Some brands offer official verification. For Lattafa, look for the 3D holographic label, scan its QR code and confirm the address is exactly verify.lattafa.com. The batch code should match on the box and the bottle, although on its own it does not prove authenticity. And an original perfume that comes without cellophane or a seal is not necessarily fake: each brand and each batch can vary. For more signs, read the guide how to tell if a perfume is authentic.

Contact

For a claim or any question, message us on WhatsApp or visit the About and contact page.
