What is a perfume decant?

Updated October 17, 2026 · By Althamir GT

A decant is a small portion of an original perfume transferred from the large bottle to a smaller container, usually a 2, 5 or 10 ml atomizer. It's the most practical way to try fragrances without paying for the full bottle.

How a decant is prepared

The perfume is transferred from the original bottle using clean tools, such as syringes, pipettes or adapters, into a small atomizer that is closed and labeled with the fragrance's name. The contents are the same perfume: only the container changes.

Advantages of buying a decant

  • Try before you commit. You can live with a scent for several days and see how it smells on your skin and how long it lasts.
  • It costs less. You pay only for the milliliters you'll use, not the full bottle.
  • More variety. For the price of one bottle you can try several fragrances.
  • It's easy to carry. A 5 or 10 ml atomizer fits in a bag or a suitcase.

How far a decant goes

A 100 ml bottle equals ten 10 ml decants. As a reference, each milliliter yields about 10 sprays, so a 10 ml decant offers around 100. With the cost-per-spray calculator you can compare what each application costs in a decant versus a full bottle.

What to check before buying one

  • That the seller uses original perfumes and clean tools.
  • That the atomizer closes well and is labeled with the fragrance's name.
  • That the container is glass, preferably dark, to protect the perfume from light.
  • That they explain how to store it; see the guide on how to store your perfumes and decants.

Decant, sample and mini: what's the difference?

A sample is usually a 1 to 2 ml vial given out by brands or stores. A mini is a small bottle made by the brand itself. A decant, on the other hand, is prepared by a seller from a large bottle, so its quality depends on the perfume being authentic and the process being clean.

How to use a decant to decide whether to buy the bottle

  1. Wear it at least three or four times, on different days and at different moments.
  2. Pay attention to how long it lasts on your skin and whether you still like it after several hours.
  3. Ask the people close to you what they think of the scent.
  4. If after a week you're still thinking about that perfume, it's a good sign.

Common myths about decants

  • “It's a diluted perfume”: no. What goes into the decant is the same perfume from the original bottle, with nothing added.
  • “The scent changes”: no, as long as it's stored properly. What can alter it is light, heat or a poorly closed container.
  • “It's only for people who can't afford the bottle”: collectors and people who rotate scents by season use them too.

When it makes sense to buy the full bottle

When you've already worn the decant on several occasions, you still like it and you'll use it often. Calculate what each spray costs you in both formats with the cost-per-spray calculator: if you wear a scent frequently, the full bottle usually works out cheaper per spray.

Frequently asked questions

Does the decant smell the same as the original bottle?

Yes. It's the same perfume; the only thing that changes is the container. If it's stored properly, it keeps its original scent.

How long does a decant last?

As long as it's tightly closed and away from light and heat, it can keep for many months without losing its characteristics. Avoid leaving it in the car or the bathroom.

What size is right for me?

The 2 or 3 ml ones are good for trying a scent for a couple of days; the 5 or 10 ml ones let you wear it at your own pace and decide whether the full bottle is worth it.
