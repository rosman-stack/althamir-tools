How to tell if a perfume is authentic

Updated October 17, 2026 · By Althamir GT

There's no foolproof test at home, but there are several signs that help you spot a counterfeit perfume before buying it.

Start with where you buy

The strongest signal is the seller: authorized stores or sellers with a track record who can explain where the product comes from. Be wary of prices far below the market: an occasional deal is normal, but a huge gap is a red flag.

Signs in the packaging

  • The cellophane or seal should look clean, even and snug.
  • The printing on the box should be sharp, with no spelling mistakes or faded colors.
  • Many brands include a batch code on the box and on the bottle; in general they should match, although the format varies by brand.
  • The bottle should have even glass, a firm cap and an atomizer that sprays a fine, even mist.

Signs in the scent

A counterfeit perfume often smells good at first but fades very quickly or feels too alcoholic. If you have a reference from another bottle or a trusted sample, compare how the scent develops over several hours.

If you buy decants

  • Ask which original bottle the decant comes from and, if possible, ask to see a photo of the bottle.
  • Check that the atomizer is labeled with the perfume's name.
  • Choose sellers who answer your questions clearly and don't pressure you to buy.

Before buying online

  • Check the seller's reviews and how long they've been operating.
  • Ask for real photos of the product and not only catalog images.
  • Prefer payment methods that offer some kind of protection.
  • Be wary of anyone who doesn't answer your questions or pressures you to pay quickly.

What a tester is

A tester is a demonstration bottle that some stores use so customers can try a scent. It's sometimes sold without a box or in simple packaging. It's not necessarily fake, but the seller should make clear what it is before you buy it.

Frequently asked questions

Does a low price always mean it's fake?

Not always, because legitimate promotions exist. But when the price is far below the usual, it's worth researching the seller before buying.

Do Arabian perfumes have a batch code?

It depends on the brand and the format; if you're not sure what your bottle should have, ask the seller or the brand.

Are Althamir's perfumes authentic?

Yes. We source only from trusted suppliers in the United States and guarantee the authenticity of what we sell.

What do I do if I suspect I bought a fake?

Keep the box and the invoice or receipt and contact the seller to ask for an explanation or a refund.
