What perfume to gift in Guatemala, by date

Updated October 22, 2026 · By Althamir GT

In Guatemala there are dates when perfume is a classic gift: Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Teacher's Day and Christmas. Getting it right depends on the person and the occasion. Here are ideas by date, with safe fragrance families for each.

February 14: Valentine's Day (Día del Cariño)

It's a date for scents with warm character: soft amber, vanilla, flowers with musk or gourmand touches. If you're not sure about your partner's taste, a set of two decants (one sweet and one fresher) lets you give a gift and discover together which one they love.

May 10: Mother's Day

Soft florals, delicate ambers and elegant vanillas work well. Avoid very strong scents if you don't know her taste. Pay attention to the perfume she already wears: giving one from the same family is usually a hit.

June 17: Father's Day

Woody, aromatic and soft spicy scents are safe choices. If he likes fresh scents, a citrus with a woody base works well during the day; if he prefers something with more presence, try an amber or a soft oud.

June 25: Teacher's Day (Día del Maestro)

For the classroom, a discreet scent is best: fresh, clean or soft woody, with few sprays. A small decant is a practical, nice gift, and it lets each person discover their favorite.

Christmas and New Year

It's the season of warm scents: amber, vanilla, spices and woods. A set of three decants (one fresh, one versatile and one for the evening) is ideal for the gatherings and posadas of December.

Tips for giving without getting it wrong

  • Order ahead: on high-demand dates, leave several days of margin for stock and delivery.
  • Notice which scents the person wears and whether they prefer fresh, sweet or woody.
  • If you don't know their taste, give a set of decants instead of a full bottle.
  • Include a note describing how each one smells, for example “fresh and citrusy, ideal for the daytime”.
  • Ask the person to take the perfume quiz and tell you the result, without spoiling the surprise.

Frequently asked questions

Is giving a decant a good idea?

Yes. It's an affordable way to give a quality perfume, especially if you don't know which one they'll like. If they love it, they can buy the full bottle.

How much should I spend?

You don't need to spend a lot. With a decant or a small set you can give a good perfume and adjust the amount to your budget.

Can I give a men's perfume to a woman, or the other way around?

Yes. Fragrance families have no gender and there are many unisex scents. What matters is that the person likes it.
