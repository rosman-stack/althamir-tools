How to store your perfumes and decants

Updated October 17, 2026 · By Althamir GT

A well-stored perfume can keep its scent for years; a poorly stored one spoils within a few months. The enemies are always the same: light, heat, humidity and air.

What damages a perfume

  • Direct light: it breaks down the aromatic components and can change the color.
  • Heat and temperature swings: they speed up evaporation and alter the scent.
  • Humidity: which is why the bathroom isn't a good place.
  • Air: every time it's opened, oxygen gets in; keep bottles tightly closed.

Where to keep them

  • In a drawer, closet or their original box, away from windows.
  • In a cool place with a stable temperature.
  • Never in the car or near the iron, the bathroom or the kitchen.

Tips for decants

  • Store them with the cap on and upright, so they don't leak.
  • If the container is transparent, keep it inside a drawer or in a dark bag.
  • If you travel, carry them in a case to avoid bumps and spills.

How to tell if it has gone bad

  • A noticeable change in color: it usually darkens.
  • A sour, metallic or overly alcoholic smell.
  • The top notes disappear and only a flat smell remains.

How to organize your collection

  • Sort it by fragrance family or by season.
  • Keep the ones you use most within reach and store the rest in their boxes.
  • Write the purchase or opening date on the box or on a label.

Before traveling

Take decants instead of large bottles: they weigh less, take up little space and, if one breaks, you don't lose all your perfume. If you fly, check the airline's liquid rules, which usually limit container sizes in carry-on luggage.

What oxidation is and why it matters

When perfume comes into contact with air, some components change chemically and the scent is altered, especially the citrus notes. That's why it's best to keep bottles tightly closed and not leave them open while you get ready.

How often to check your perfumes

Every so often, smell the ones you rarely use: if you notice a change in smell or color, stop using them. Decants, having more air in proportion to the remaining liquid, can change sooner if left half-full for a long time.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an unopened perfume last?

Well stored, it can last several years. Citrus scents tend to change sooner than woody or oriental ones.

Can I keep it in the refrigerator?

It isn't necessary if you keep it in a cool, dark place, and sudden temperature changes can affect it.

What do I do if my decant changed smell or color?

If the change is strong, it's better not to use it on your skin.
