Decants in Guatemala: how to buy with confidence

Updated October 22, 2026 · By Althamir GT

In Guatemala, many decants are sold through Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. That makes hard-to-find perfumes easier to get, but it also means you need to buy carefully. This guide gives you a practical checklist for choosing where and how to buy.

Why decants make sense here

Most perfumes available in the country are imported, so choosing the wrong full bottle is a big investment. With a decant you can try the scent on your skin, in your climate and in your routine before deciding whether the full bottle is worth it.

Six questions to ask before you pay

  1. Which original bottle does the decant come from? A serious seller answers with the exact name and, if you ask, shows you a photo or video of the bottle.
  2. What container and size does it come in? Normally a glass atomizer of 2, 5 or 10 ml, closed and labeled with the fragrance's name.
  3. How are the decants prepared? They should use clean tools and original perfumes; see our guide on how to tell if a perfume is authentic.
  4. What happens if it arrives spilled or damaged? Ask about their policy before you pay.
  5. How and when is it delivered, and how much is shipping? Confirm whether they deliver to your city or department and who handles the delivery.
  6. Which payment methods do they accept? The clearer and more traceable, the better.

How to pay more safely

  • If you can, prefer a card payment through a payment gateway: it leaves a record and usually offers more protection than a direct transfer.
  • Be wary of anyone who demands 100% upfront, has no track record or reviews, and gets nervous when you ask questions.
  • Keep screenshots of the conversation, the listing and the payment receipt.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes with anyone who asks for them to “confirm” the purchase.

What to check when your order arrives

  • The atomizer should be closed, leak-free and labeled with the fragrance's name.
  • The container should be glass and packed to survive the trip.
  • The scent should match what you ordered. If you have doubts, try it on your skin and compare it with a reliable reference.
  • Store it away from heat and light; in the car or the bathroom it spoils faster. More in how to store your perfumes and decants.

What an order at Althamir GT looks like

  1. You choose your decants in the shop, in 5 ml or 10 ml sizes, and add them to the cart.
  2. You send your order over WhatsApp or pay by card from the shop.
  3. You can check your order status in the “My orders” section using your phone number.
  4. If you're looking for a perfume that isn't in the catalog, message us and we'll look into it.

Prices, stock and delivery times are always confirmed in the shop or over WhatsApp, because they change.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to buy decants through social media?

It can be, if the seller answers clearly, has a track record and reviews, and offers a traceable way to pay. If anything feels off, don't pay.

Which size should I order first?

To decide whether you like a scent, 5 ml is enough for several days. If you already know you'll wear it often, 10 ml goes further.

Can I request a perfume that isn't in the shop?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the perfume's name and we'll tell you whether we can get it.
