About Althamir GT

Althamir GT is a decant and Arabian perfume shop in Guatemala. We work with Arabian and niche fragrances so you can try them in small portions before committing to a full bottle.

Why we built these tools

Choosing a perfume online isn't easy. That's why we built three free tools: a quiz to find your ideal scent, a calculator to see what each spray really costs, and a guide to combining fragrances (layering). They run right in your browser and you don't need to sign up.

Original perfumes

We source only from trusted suppliers in the United States and guarantee the authenticity of the perfumes we sell. If you have any doubt about a product, message us and we'll help. You can also read our guide on how to tell if a perfume is authentic.

Contact

You can message us on WhatsApp with questions, decant orders, or to request a perfume you don't see in the shop; we personalize every order.
